# ATLAS-001

## Research Question

How do computers represent engineering knowledge and reasoning today?

---

## Why This Matters

Engineering decisions combine evidence, assumptions, models, simulations, constraints, uncertainty, and human judgment.

Atlas seeks to understand how current computational systems represent these elements and where important information may be lost.

---

## Scope

This investigation covers:

- Knowledge Representation
- Knowledge Graphs
- Ontologies
- Rule-Based Systems
- Engineering Traceability
- Decision Support Systems
- Provenance
- Digital Twins (representation only)

---

## Deliverable

A map of the current state of the field.

No proposed solutions.
