# H-003: Confidence Propagation

## Statement

Engineering software represents models and data well, but it rarely represents how confidence in decisions changes as evidence and assumptions evolve.

## Research Question

Can confidence be propagated through a reasoning graph in a mathematically consistent and explainable way?

## Why It Matters

Engineers make decisions under uncertainty every day.

Most software reports outputs.

Very little software reports how much those outputs should be trusted as assumptions change.

## Success Criteria

Develop and validate at least one confidence propagation algorithm on realistic engineering case studies.
