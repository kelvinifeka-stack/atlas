## D-001

Decision:
Atlas will optimize for discovering true computational principles rather than pursuing novelty.

Reason:
Novel ideas are valuable only if they better explain reality or solve real problems.

Implication:
Every research direction must be evaluated against evidence, existing theory, and practical usefulness before implementation.

Status:
Accepted

## D-002

Decision:
Atlas will follow a fixed research algorithm for every investigation.

Algorithm:
Observe → Measure → Model → Challenge → Formalize → Prototype → Reflect

Reason:
A consistent method reduces bias, improves reproducibility, and makes progress measurable.

Status:
Accepted
